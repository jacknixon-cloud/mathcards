About Math Cards

Math Cards is an educational game that helps children practice and improve their mathematics skills through engaging gameplay. Problems are presented as colorful playing cards with a hand-drawn crayon aesthetic, making learning math approachable and fun!

Pricing

Addition mode is FREE forever! Try it out and see how much fun math practice can be.

Unlock all 9 additional game modes with a monthly subscription and get access to multiplication, division, and all fraction modes.

Game Modes

  • Addition - Build fundamental skills (FREE)
  • Subtraction - Practice taking away numbers (Subscription)
  • Multiplication Tables - Practice times tables 1-10 (Subscription)
  • Division Tables - Practice division with visual dot representations (Subscription)
  • Simplify Fractions - Reduce fractions to lowest terms (Subscription)
  • Multiply Fractions - Multiply two fractions together (Subscription)
  • Divide Fractions - Divide fractions with animated reciprocal visualization (Subscription)
  • Compare Fractions - Identify the larger of two fractions (Subscription)
  • Add Fractions - Add two fractions together (Subscription)
  • Subtract Fractions - Subtract one fraction from another (Subscription)

Key Features

  • Difficulty Levels - Easy, Medium, and Hard options for fraction modes
  • Work Pad Feature - Step-by-step guided problem-solving for fraction operations
  • Hall of Fame - Track your progress with leaderboards for each game mode
  • Instant Feedback - Visual and audio responses to reinforce learning
  • Offline Play - Works without internet connection
