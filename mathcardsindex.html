About Math Cards

Math Cards is an educational app that helps children practice and improve their mathematics skills through engaging gameplay. Problems are presented as colorful playing cards with a hand-drawn crayon aesthetic, making learning math approachable and fun!

Pricing

Addition mode is FREE forever! Try it out and see how much fun math practice can be.

Unlock all 9 additional game modes with a monthly subscription and get access to subtraction, multiplication, division, and all six fraction modes.

For Schools & Classrooms

Math Cards supports bulk school licenses through a simple code system — no app store purchases needed for each device.

  • School Codes — Redeem a single bulk code on any classroom device to instantly unlock all game modes.
  • Student Name Entry — Each student enters their own name at the start of every session, so scores are tracked individually even on a shared device.
  • Class Report — Teachers tap the Report button to see a live session log showing every student's name, score, and the questions they answered incorrectly.
  • Filter by Date & Device — Narrow the report to a specific day or classroom device using dropdown filters. The date list automatically shows only the current school year (September through July).
  • Export to Spreadsheet — One tap copies the report as a table you can paste directly into Excel or Google Sheets.

Game Modes

  • Addition - Build fundamental skills (FREE)
  • Subtraction - Practice taking away numbers (Subscription)
  • Multiplication Tables - Practice times tables 1-10 (Subscription)
  • Division Tables - Practice division with visual dot representations (Subscription)
  • Simplify Fractions - Reduce fractions to lowest terms (Subscription)
  • Multiply Fractions - Multiply two fractions together (Subscription)
  • Divide Fractions - Divide fractions with animated reciprocal visualization (Subscription)
  • Compare Fractions - Identify the larger of two fractions (Subscription)
  • Add Fractions - Add two fractions together (Subscription)
  • Subtract Fractions - Subtract one fraction from another (Subscription)

Key Features

  • Work Pad - For fraction operations, the Work Pad provides step-by-step scaffolding that shows HOW to solve problems, not just the answer. It breaks down the process by finding the GCD, LCD, simplifying, and more. Solutions auto-populate from the Work Pad directly to the answer fields, so students can choose to solve independently or use the guidance.
  • Customizable Difficulty - For addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division modes, select which number table to practice (e.g., focus on 6s and 7s). Fraction modes feature Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels.
  • Positive Reinforcement Loop - Math Cards is designed to reinforce learning through quick repetition and positive reinforcement. After answering each problem, students get instant visual and audio feedback. There's minimal negativity in the experience—the focus is on progress and improvement. Once you see your score on the leaderboard, you can start again immediately and try to beat it, creating an exciting cycle of problem-solving, scoring, and climbing the leaderboard.
  • Hall of Fame - Per-mode leaderboard tracks each player's personal best scores
  • Classroom Reports - Teachers get a live session log showing every student's score and mistakes, filterable by date and device
  • Offline Play - Game modes work without an internet connection (reports require connectivity)
