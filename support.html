Help & Support

How to Play

Math Cards is an interactive educational game designed for kids ages 7-13. Simply select a game mode, choose your difficulty level, and start solving math problems. Earn points for correct answers and climb the leaderboard! Use the Work Pad feature to show your work step-by-step.

Game Modes

Math Cards features 8 different game modes to help you master various math skills:

1. Multiplication Tables

Practice multiplication facts from basic to advanced levels.

2. Division Tables

Master division skills with problems ranging from simple to complex.

3. Fractions - Adding

Learn to add fractions with like and unlike denominators.

4. Fractions - Subtracting

Practice subtracting fractions with increasing difficulty.

5. Fractions - Multiplying

Understand fraction multiplication through interactive problems.

6. Fractions - Dividing

Master the art of dividing fractions with step-by-step guidance.

7. Fractions - Simplifying

Learn to reduce fractions to their simplest form.

8. Fractions - Converting

Convert between fractions, decimals, and percentages.

Work Pad Feature

The Work Pad is a built-in tool that allows you to show your mathematical work step-by-step. This feature helps you:

  • Work through problems methodically
  • Keep track of your calculations
  • Learn the proper way to solve problems
  • Review your work before submitting

Simply tap the Work Pad icon during any problem to open it and write out your solution step-by-step.

Scoring System

Math Cards uses a fair and transparent scoring system:

  • Correct Answer: +10 points (base score)
  • Speed Bonus: Extra points for fast answers in Time Attack mode
  • Streak Bonus: Multiplier for consecutive correct answers
  • Difficulty Bonus: Higher points for harder difficulty levels

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I play offline?

A: Yes! Math Cards works completely offline. All game data is stored locally on your device.

Q: How do I access the Hall of Fame?

A: Your scores are automatically tracked. Visit the Hall of Fame section from the main menu to see the top scores for each game mode and difficulty level.

Q: Can I reset my scores?

A: Yes, you can reset your scores from the settings menu. Please note that this action cannot be undone.

Q: What age group is Math Cards for?

A: Math Cards is designed for children ages 7-13, though it can be enjoyed by anyone wanting to practice math skills.

Troubleshooting

The app won't load

Try closing and reopening the app. If the problem persists, try restarting your device.

Scores aren't saving

Check that your device has sufficient storage space. If you're still having issues, contact support.

The Work Pad feature isn't working

Make sure you have the latest version of Math Cards installed. Update through your app store if needed.

Performance issues

Close other applications running in the background to improve performance. Also, consider clearing the app cache from your device settings.
