Math Cards Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

Data Collection

Math Cards does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All game data (scores and leaderboards) is stored locally on your device only.

Subscription Information

Math Cards offers a monthly subscription to unlock all game modes beyond the free Addition mode. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID and App Store account. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Children's Privacy

Math Cards is designed for children and does not collect any personal information from users of any age. We are committed to protecting children's privacy and comply with applicable children's privacy laws.
