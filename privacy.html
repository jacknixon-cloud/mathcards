Math Cards Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 17, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use Math Cards, and tells you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

Data Collection

Math Cards does not collect personal information by default. All game data (scores and leaderboards) is stored locally on your device only. The only exception is if you optionally provide your email address for purchase recovery purposes (see below).

Optional Email Collection

Math Cards offers an optional feature to save your email address solely for the purpose of recovering your subscription or purchases if you lose access to your Apple ID. Providing your email is entirely voluntary — the app works fully without it.

If you choose to provide your email:

  • It is used only to help you recover your purchase
  • It is not shared with third parties
  • It is not used for marketing or promotional purposes
  • You may request deletion at any time by contacting us at mathcardsapp@gmail.com

Usage Data

Usage data may be collected automatically, either generated by the use of the Service or from the Service infrastructure itself (for example, the duration of a page visit). This data is used only to monitor and improve the Service.

Subscription Information

Math Cards offers a monthly subscription to unlock all game modes beyond the free Addition mode. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID and App Store account. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Children's Privacy

Math Cards is designed for children and is committed to protecting children's privacy. The app does not collect any personal information by default. If the optional email feature is used, it should be provided by a parent or guardian on behalf of the child. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including COPPA.

Use of Data

We use any collected data only for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service
  • To help you recover your purchase if requested
  • To notify you about changes to our Service
  • To provide customer support
  • To gather analysis so that we can improve the Service
  • To monitor the usage of the Service
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

Email: mathcardsapp@gmail.com
