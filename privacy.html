Math Cards Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 3, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use Math Cards, and tells you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

Data Collection

Math Cards does not collect personal information by default. All game data (scores and leaderboards) is stored locally on your device only. The only exception is your email address, which is collected by Stripe when you make a purchase through our web checkout. School and classroom accounts are subject to additional data collection as described below.

School and Classroom Use

When Math Cards is accessed using a bulk school redemption code, the following data is collected and stored:

  • A name entered by the student before gameplay
  • A device name assigned by the teacher at setup
  • Game session data including score and incorrect answers
  • Timestamp of each session

This data is stored securely and is only accessible to the teacher or administrator who redeemed the school code. It is used solely to provide classroom progress reporting. No student data is collected for individual or trial redemption codes.

School accounts are responsible for obtaining appropriate consent from parents or guardians before students enter their names into the app, in compliance with COPPA and FERPA.

Email Collection

If you purchase a subscription via our web checkout, your email address is collected by Stripe as part of the payment process. We may use this email address to send you a short survey to gather feedback about your experience with Math Cards. We will not use your email for any other purpose, and we will not share it with any third parties.

Stripe Payments

Web-based purchases are processed by Stripe. Stripe may collect and store payment and personal information in accordance with their own privacy policy. We do not store your payment details. You can review Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

Usage Data

Usage data may be collected automatically, either generated by the use of the Service or from the Service infrastructure itself (for example, the duration of a page visit). This data is used only to monitor and improve the Service.

Subscription Information

Math Cards offers a subscription to unlock all game modes beyond the free Addition mode. Subscriptions can be managed through your Apple ID and App Store account, or via our web checkout powered by Stripe.

Children's Privacy

Math Cards is designed for children and is committed to protecting children's privacy. The app does not collect any personal information by default. Any purchase made on behalf of a child should be completed by a parent or guardian. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including COPPA. For school accounts, the responsibility for obtaining parental consent rests with the school or teacher administering the account.
